Step 03 — Backprop

Gradients flow backward

Chain rule: propagate ∂L/∂· from output back to input.

1

Output gradient

∂L/∂ŷ = ŷ − y
2

Through FC3

∂L/∂h₂ = (∂L/∂ŷ) · W₃
3

Through ReLU

∂L/∂z₂ = ∂L/∂h₂ ⊙ 𝟙[z₂ > 0]
4

Weight gradients

∂L/∂W₂ = (∂L/∂z₂) ⊗ h₁
5

Updating the weights
